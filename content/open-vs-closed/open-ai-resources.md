# Resources & Glossary

This page collects the "why it matters" discussion, a set of clear next steps, a curated (mostly free and open-source) resource shelf, and a glossary you can dip into when jargon appears.

## Why this matters

- **Democratization.** Open-source means AI isn't controlled by a handful of corporations — anyone, anywhere can use and build on it.
- **Innovation.** Open models accelerate research globally, especially where access to paid tools is limited.
- **Bias & safety.** Transparency lets people find and fix harmful biases. You can't fix a black box.
- **Privacy & sustainability.** Running models locally keeps data at home and reduces lock-in.
- **Ethics.** Should the most powerful technology ever created be owned by a few, or accessible to all?

> **Question to ponder:** who should control the most powerful technology in history?

## What's next, by level

| Level                   | Action                                                               |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Absolute beginner**   | Play with [Hugging Face Spaces](https://huggingface.co/spaces) to see models in action — no code. |
| **Curious professional** | Try a no-code tool ([Claude](https://claude.ai/) / [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/)) to feel what a model does. |
| **Aspirational builder** | Explore a free short course (list below) or [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/). |
| **Advanced explorer**   | [Fine-tune an open model](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers) using Colab's free GPUs, or run one locally with [Ollama](https://ollama.com/). |

## Curated resource shelf (mostly free / FOSS)

**Understand the technology**
- [DeepLearning.AI — How Transformer LLMs Work](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/how-transformer-llms-work/) — free short course.
- [3Blue1Brown — Attention in Transformers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMlx5fFNoYc) — visual explainer.
- [Hugging Face — NLP course](https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course) — free, hands-on.

**Explore open-source**
- [Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/models) — search free models.
- [Hugging Face Spaces](https://huggingface.co/spaces) — try models without code.
- [Papers with Code](https://paperswithcode.com/) — see cutting-edge research with open code.
- [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) — run open models locally.

**The open vs closed debate**
- [Stanford HAI — AI Index Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index) — the big annual report.
- [EleutherAI — "Open Source AI is not a silver bullet"](https://blog.eleuther.ai/open-source-ai-not-a-silver-bullet/) — a balanced, critical view.

**Stay updated**
- [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/) — DeepLearning.AI's weekly newsletter.
- [Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/) — weekly AI news.
- [Hugging Face Weekly](https://huggingface.co/blog) — community updates.

## Glossary

| Term          | Plain-English meaning                                             |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Transformer** | The architecture behind all modern AI models (2017, "Attention Is All You Need"). |
| **Attention** | How a model focuses on the words that matter most in a sentence.  |
| **Weights**   | The "knowledge" a model has learned — a giant set of numbers.      |
| **Token**     | A piece of text (a word, subword or character) the model reads.    |
| **API**       | A way to use a model over the internet without owning it.          |
| **Fine-tuning** | Adapting a pre-trained model to a specific task.                 |

## Final takeaway

> You don't need to build AI to benefit from it — but understanding it ensures you're not just a passive consumer.
