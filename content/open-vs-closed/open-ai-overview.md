# Proprietary vs Open-Source AI Models

> **A reference, not a tutorial.** This series is designed to accompany a talk session — each page is a set of ideas with links and light explanations you can skim now and revisit later. Lean on the practical, free and open-source (FOSS) resources.

You use AI every day — ChatGPT, auto-complete, voice assistants. But do you know **who owns the brain** behind them? That's the question this series answers.

After a model is trained, its creators make one big decision: **Do we share this, or keep it secret?** That single choice shapes how you can use it, what it costs, what it reveals, and who benefits.

By the end, you'll understand the difference between **proprietary** (closed) and **open-source** models — and you'll have hands-on, free resources to try the open ones yourself.
